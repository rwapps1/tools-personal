🎧 Podcast Player

Paste or upload a script and tap Play — it's read aloud using your phone's own voice, live. Nothing to download, nothing to wait for.

Speed 1.0x

Keep this tab open and your phone unlocked while it plays — most phones stop background tabs, so this won't play like a music app in the background or with the screen off. If it cuts out partway through on Android Chrome, that's a known browser quirk with long readings; Stop and Play again picks up better on a shorter chunk, or just try once more. Voice options come from your phone/browser, so what's listed varies by device.
